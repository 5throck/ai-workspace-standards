## Context

Use in Phase 2 (Design) whenever an engagement requires organizational structure, job architecture, or workforce/headcount planning work, and in Phase 3 when restructuring or voluntary-retirement process design linked to outplacement support is in scope. Owned by the Org Design Consultant, who synthesizes HRM/HRD and labor-relations specialist inputs into a coherent structural design.

## When to Use

- When designing or redesigning an organizational structure
- When defining job families, job levels, or role definitions
- When headcount/workforce planning is needed for a current-state or target-state model
- When a business-reason-based restructuring or voluntary-retirement process must be designed

## Execution Steps

1. **Define Organizational Design Principles**: Establish the design principles against the engagement's strategic goals:
   - Span of control (target reports per manager, by level)
   - Number of layers (target organizational depth)
   - Centralization vs. decentralization of key decision rights
   - Document the rationale linking each principle to a strategic goal

2. **Job Architecture Design**:
   - Define job families (functional groupings of related work)
   - Define job levels within each family (a leveling ladder, not individual titles)
   - Write role definitions per family/level — distinguish these from individual job descriptions, which describe a specific incumbent's day-to-day tasks
   - Cross-check the architecture against the design principles from Step 1 (e.g., does the leveling ladder support the target span of control?)

3. **Workforce/Headcount Planning by Scenario**:
   - Current-state baseline: headcount by job family/level/department today
   - Target-state model: headcount by job family/level/department under the new design
   - Gap analysis: surplus/shortfall by family/level, with net headcount delta
   - Sensitivity: model at least a conservative and an aggressive scenario when the target state is uncertain

4. **Restructuring Process Design** (when applicable):
   - Confirm business-reason basis for the restructuring before designing selection mechanics
   - Selection criteria fairness safeguards: document objective, non-discriminatory selection criteria and how they will be applied consistently
   - Sequencing: order of announcement, consultation, selection, and implementation steps
   - Linkage to voluntary-retirement and outplacement support scope already owned by the Org Design Consultant — ensure the restructuring plan explicitly connects redeployment criteria to the voluntary-retirement program terms and outplacement handoff
   - Flag any step requiring Labor Relations Specialist or Labor Compliance Analyst review before execution

5. **Risk Review**: For any restructuring or headcount reduction scenario, log risks (legal, reputational, morale) in the restructuring risk register.

## Output Format

- **Org Structure Chart Description + Rationale**: narrative description of the proposed structure and how it satisfies the design principles
- **Job Architecture Table**: Job Family, Level, Role Definition
- **Headcount Plan by Scenario**: Family/Level/Department, Current-State, Target-State, Gap
- **Restructuring Risk Register** (when applicable): Risk, Category (legal/reputational/morale), Likelihood, Mitigation Action, Owner
