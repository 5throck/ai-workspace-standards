## Style notes for calibration

- **Headline register**: uses a "[Company Watch]" section tag plus a market-positioning framing ("leads refiners in operating profit") rather than a plain YoY percentage — denser, assumes the reader already knows the refiner peer set and doesn't need it re-explained. This matches SKILL.md's description of TheBell as assuming reader sophistication and using market shorthand without explanation (step 4).
- **Disclosure-citation phrasing**: a terse, filing-date-anchored attribution (English gloss: "disclosed on the 10th") — same underlying pattern as the Sedaily samples but with less scene-setting around it.
- **Comparative framing over standalone figures**: the lead fact is not just the absolute profit number but the multi-year competitive ranking built from it ("second consecutive year, following 2024") — TheBell pieces in this corpus tend to synthesize a single filing into a peer/trend narrative rather than reporting the number in isolation, a useful contrast against the Sedaily samples' more literal figure-first ledger style.
