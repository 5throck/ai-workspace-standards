## 목적

Guides fact-checker through the newsroom's source-verification discipline — every material claim in an article must be traceable to 2+ independently verifiable sources before publish. Use when: after financial-analyst and legal-researcher produce their briefs, before the reporter drafts the article; re-run after style-editor's rewrite pass to confirm no figures drifted.
