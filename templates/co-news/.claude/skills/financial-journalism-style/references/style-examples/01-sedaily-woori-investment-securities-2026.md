## Representative excerpt (paraphrase-safe summary of reported facts, not a verbatim reproduction)

Per the Sedaily report: Woori Investment & Securities' H1 net operating revenue reached KRW 146.2bn, up 58.7% YoY; non-interest income rose 110.0% to KRW 83.9bn, lifting the non-interest share of revenue from 43% (H1 last year) to 58% this year. IB-related fee income alone rose 206% to KRW 49.1bn. The company attributed the acceleration to large-deal mandates following its capital raise. Operating profit rose 93.3% YoY to KRW 29.4bn; net income rose 47.1% to KRW 24.7bn.
