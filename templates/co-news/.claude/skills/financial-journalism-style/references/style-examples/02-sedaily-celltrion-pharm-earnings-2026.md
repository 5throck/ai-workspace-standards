## Style notes for calibration

- **Headline**: superlative framing tied directly to the disclosed figure ("record quarterly operating profit") rather than a bare percentage — shows Sedaily headlines sometimes lead with a qualitative record-breaking claim as long as it is grounded in the disclosed number, not just raw YoY math.
- **Disclosure-citation phrasing**: the piece explicitly frames the numbers as coming from a same-day provisional-earnings disclosure filed that day (English gloss: "the company disclosed provisional results on the 4th, stating that...") — this is the exact "according to the filing" / "the company said" pattern the skill's step 3 (Korean-mode disclosure-citation phrasing) describes, and directly informs how co-news should phrase DART-sourced figures in Korean output.
- **Compactness**: this class of short same-day disclosure writeup is terse — one or two sentences carrying the headline number, filing date, and comparison point — useful as the model for co-news's own DART-triggered short-form stories (as opposed to the longer analytical piece in Sample 01).
