## 1. Purpose
Systematically prevent (a) methane (CH₄) gas lower-explosive-limit (LEL) explosion, (b) leachate chemical hazard, (c) slope collapse, and (d) landfill-fire firefighting-response hazards at landfill and anaerobic-digestion (AD) facilities by applying the hierarchy of controls. Korea's Sudokwon Landfill is the world's largest by volume; methane generated by anaerobic decomposition in the landfill mass presents an explosion hazard when atmospheric concentration reaches 5% LEL, and subsurface migration has caused adjacent-structure explosion incidents. This workflow supports the Safety & Health Manager's general safety-assurance duty (중대재해처벌법/SAPA Article 4), the risk-assessment duty (산업안전보건법/OSHA-KR Article 36), and the waste-treatment-business licensing duty (폐기물관리법/WCA Article 25).

This is an **industry-unique workflow** — a waste-industry-specific atmospheric-methane-hazard management procedure that does not duplicate any workflow under `workflows/_shared/`. Incinerator/shredder maintenance LOTO is covered by `incinerator-shredder-loto`; sewage/manhole H₂S asphyxiation prevention is covered by `sewage-confined-h2d-prevent` (this workflow manages CH₄ atmospheric hazard, a physically distinct mechanism from H₂S asphyxiation).

## 2. Scope
- **Industry**: Waste (code: `waste`, landfill and anaerobic-digestion operations)
- **Work covered**: Landfill-cell operation and daily cover, gas estimation and monitoring, gas-collection / flare / utilization plant operation, leachate collection and treatment, slope-stability inspection, AD tank and power-generation equipment operation and maintenance, landfill-fire response
- **Trigger points**: New landfill-cell commissioning, sharp change in gas-estimation volume, LEL alarm response, leachate leak, slope settlement or cracking, post-earthquake / heavy-rainfall inspection, AD-tank cleaning and entry work

## 4. Procedure
1. **Risk assessment**: Identify per-cell and per-AD-tank methane generation volumes and subsurface-migration pathways, leachate chemical composition, slope stability (angle, groundwater level), and ignition sources (heavy equipment, static electricity, open flame). Aligns with 산업안전보건법 (OSHA-KR) Article 36 (risk assessment).
2. **Apply the hierarchy of controls**:
   1. **Elimination**: Engineered geomembrane liner and leachate-collection system to cut off subsurface migration at the source; pre-reduction of methane concentration via gas collection and flare system; active and passive venting.
   2. **Engineering**: Gas-extraction well network, flare, gas-engine power-generation plant; surface / subsurface / building LEL gas-detection network; slope-stability engineering (berms, benched gradients); leachate-leak detection; explosion-proof electrical equipment.
   3. **Administrative**: Comply with 폐기물관리법 (WCA) Article 25 license conditions; maintain a landfill-fire response plan under 소방기본법 (BFS) Article 16; retain the 화학물질관리법 (CCA) Article 23 accident-preparedness-substance management plan (covers AD facilities); issue safe-work permits (confined space / fire / heavy equipment); conduct shift-change TBM; control ignition sources (smoking ban, hot-work permit); periodically measure gas concentration.
   4. **PPE**: Personal multi-gas monitor (CH₄/LEL/O₂/H₂S), flame-resistant clothing for hot-work, respiratory protection for AD-tank entry, hard hat and safety boots (last resort).
3. **Methane LEL control**: Continuously monitor surface, subsurface, and adjacent-structure gas concentrations; on reaching 25% LEL, evacuate personnel, increase ventilation, eliminate ignition sources, and operate flare/extraction to lower concentration. Aligns with 폐기물관리법 (WCA) Article 13 (waste-treatment standards) and 소방기본법 (BFS) Article 16 (fire/explosion response).
4. **Leachate control**: Inspect leachate-collection and treatment train operation; on leak detection, contain spread and execute decontamination; periodically analyze leachate chemical composition. Field application of the risk-assessment duty (Art 36) and safety-measures duty (Art 38).
5. **Slope-stability control**: Enforce cell-slope angle and daily-cover rules; inspect for settlement and cracking after heavy rainfall or earthquake; restrict heavy-equipment access. Aligns with 산업안전보건기준에관한규칙 (OSHSR) Ch.6 §2 (collapse prevention) under OSHA-KR Article 38.
6. **Landfill-fire response**: On landfill fire, minimize additional oxygen supply (extend cover), halt flare operation (prevent flashback), select extinguishing media (water spray, inert gas), and dispatch the fire service. Respond under 소방기본법 (BFS) Article 16 (firefighting operations). Deep-seated fires require a long-duration extinguishment and cooling plan.
7. **Emergency response**: Pre-establish procedures for immediate evacuation, rescue, and inter-agency notification on methane explosion, slope collapse, or mass-leachate leak. For AD-tank entry, apply confined-space-entry permit (pre-measurement of oxygen and gas concentration).
8. **Recordkeeping & audit**: Generate the evidence record (§5); retain gas-concentration logs, leachate-analysis results, slope-inspection records, safe-work permits, fire-response drill records, and incident / near-miss records.

## 7. Regulatory Notes
Landfill and anaerobic-digestion operations are governed primarily by the 폐기물관리법 (WCA), which regulates treatment standards (Art 13) and waste-treatment-business licensing and operation (Art 25). Adjacent anchors: 소방기본법 (BFS — natural anchor for landfill-fire and methane-explosion firefighting response, Art 16) and 화학물질관리법 (CCA — accident-preparedness-substance management plan and accident-preparedness duties, Art 23; applicable where the AD facility handles accident-preparedness substances). **Correction note — CCA vs ARECA**: this workflow's `schema.yaml` legal_basis cites `화학물질관리법 Article 23` (CCA, 사고대비물질). This is a distinct statute from `화학물질의 등록 및 평가 등에 관한 법률` (ARECA / K-REACH). The accident-preparedness-and-response duty for the methane and accident-preparedness substances at landfill and AD facilities finds its natural anchor in CCA Article 23; this corrected citation was confirmed by the compliance-agent via live MCP verification. 산업안전보건법 (OSHA-KR) provides the general basis for the risk-assessment (Art 36), incident-recording (Art 57), and collapse-prevention (Art 100) duties. Whereas the existing `incinerator-shredder-loto` workflow addresses in-equipment byproduct gas during incineration/pyrolysis, this workflow addresses the physically distinct atmospheric methane hazard of landfills (subsurface migration, deep-seated fire).

## 8. Outsourcing Note
Landfill and AD-facility operations run on a multi-tier contracting structure — waste haulers, leachate-treatment O&M contractors, gas-extraction and flare-plant operators, heavy-equipment leasing and operating firms — making 중대재해처벌법 (SAPA) Article 5 (outsourcing/contract safety obligation) especially relevant. The commissioning client (the landfill operator) must ensure haulers, equipment O&M subcontractors, and heavy-equipment contractors apply this workflow's controls (LEL monitoring, ignition-source control, confined-space-entry procedures, slope-stability rules) down the contracting chain.
