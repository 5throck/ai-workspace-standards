## 목적

Plan rolling-stock depot maintenance safety — EMU/coach/locomotive vehicle-movement lockout (wheel chock + derail + brake scotch), bogey heavy-lift rigging, undercarriage pit work, and fall protection from rolling-stock roof. Vehicle-LOTO distinct from fixed-plant LOTO. RSA Art 48 + OSHA Art 92 (LOTO 정지) + OSHA Art 99 (추락 방지 — uncited by existing railway WFs) + SAPA Art 4.
