## 목적

Apply GHS Rev 9 (2021) classification rules to chemical substances and mixtures per OSHA-KR Article 104 (유해인자의 분류기준; 산업안전보건법 시행규칙 별표 18). Outputs hazard classes, categories, H/P-Statements, label elements.

- **스코프(scope)**: `co-safety`
- **버전**: 1.0.0"
