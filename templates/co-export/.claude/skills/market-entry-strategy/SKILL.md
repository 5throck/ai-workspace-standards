## Context

Use in Phase 1 (research) and Phase 3 (synthesis/strategy production) whenever the engagement
requires a go-to-market recommendation, not just compliance clearance. A strategically ideal
market is worthless if the compliance picture blocks it — this skill's synthesis step exists to
prevent that failure mode.

## When to Use

- Client is evaluating whether/how to enter a specific overseas market
- Client needs candidate buyers or distribution partners identified
- Multiple entry channel options need trade-off comparison
- Compliance findings (tariff cost, FTA rate, export control status) need to be folded into an
  economics-aware market recommendation

## Execution Steps

1. **Market Demand Assessment**: Evaluate destination-market demand trends for the product
   category using trade statistics (UN Comtrade, destination customs import data) and industry
   reporting — cite sources and dates.
2. **Competitive Landscape Analysis**: Identify existing competitors (Korean and local) and
   typical price positioning in the target market.
3. **Entry Channel Comparison**: Compare direct export, distributor/agent partnership,
   e-commerce marketplace, and local incorporation on cost, control, and speed to market.
4. **Buyer/Distributor Discovery** (when requested): Identify candidates via trade show
   directories, KOTRA buyer databases, and industry association listings; screen by company size,
   product portfolio fit, and import history. Present as a screened candidate list requiring the
   client's own due diligence — never as vetted business partners.
5. **Pull In Regulatory Context**: Cite `foreign-regulatory-intelligence-analyst` findings for any
   destination-market registration/certification requirement — do not determine this
   independently.
6. **Synthesize With Compliance Findings**: Integrate tariff cost (from
   `hs-classification-specialist`), FTA preferential rate (from `fta-origin-analyst`), and control
   clearance (from `export-control-compliance-specialist`) into the overall market-entry
   economics before finalizing a recommendation.
7. **Handoff**: Pass the approved entry plan to `trade-documentation-specialist` and
   `logistics-coordinator` once Phase 2 gate clears.

## Output Format

- Market entry strategy document: demand summary, competitive landscape, entry channel comparison
  with trade-offs, buyer/distributor candidate list (if requested), integrated recommendation
- Client-facing presentation summarizing the recommendation
