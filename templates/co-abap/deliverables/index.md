# Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)

Central index for `deliverables/REQ-NNN-[slug]/` requirement folders.

Rows are appended by `bun scripts/co-abap/new-requirement.ts "<title>"` at Stage 1 with
status `Draft`. The Stage, QA Gate, and Release columns advance through Stages 1–5 — see
`docs/phase-definitions.md` and `AGENTS.md` §3.3 for the stage ownership model.
