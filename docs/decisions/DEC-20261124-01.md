## Context

The unified 03:00 batch runner produced thirteen fleet PRs on 2026-11-24 and
merged none of them. Two independent holds applied:

- **Protected paths.** Every PR touches `memory/**`, because each project's
  dev-sync commits its own session log and retention prune. The seven delivery
  PRs also touch `scripts/audit.ts`, `scripts/dev-sync.ts`, `scripts/hooks/**`,
  `AGENTS.md` and `.githooks/commit-msg`. Under `docs/protected-paths.json`
  these are hold-for-human-merge paths.
- **No CI verdict.** The fleet repos other than co-abap are private. Every
  check on them failed in about three seconds with `runner_id: 0` and
  `steps: 0`, the shape of the account billing wall first recorded on
  2026-11-21. A probe re-run at 2026-11-23T23:11:59Z failed identically.

The billing-wall exception of the merge predicate has been suspended since
the 2026-11-20 meeting pending explicit owner re-authorization. Its text also
requires exception merges to stay inside a wave whose canary merged CLEAN.

## Decision

The owner's instruction is recorded as the human merge decision that the
protected-path hold was waiting for, and as a one-time, scoped authorization
to merge PRs whose checks never started under the conditions listed in the
frontmatter.

One substitution is made explicit. The suspended exception's canary clause
requires the canary to merge CLEAN, which no private-repo PR can do while the
wall stands. For this authorization only, "canary merged CLEAN" is replaced by
"canary merged first, with a green exit-code battery on its head and no
regression in its post-merge verification". co-deck #259 is that canary. It is
a PARTIAL delivery: the engine preserved ten conflict-classified files and the
`humanize` manifest entry was withheld, as recorded in the PR body. It is
merged as such and is not recorded as a successful delivery.

co-consult #134 is not merged. It is a work-preservation PR whose tracked
change (deletion of `memory/2026-11-13.md`) is already on `main` through
PR #135; it is closed as superseded.

## Consequences

- Up to twelve fleet merges carry `test-integrity: unverified` in the run
  ledger. The first real CI run on each of those repositories is the
  validation of record for them.
- The nightly runner's merge predicate is unchanged. Its billing-wall
  exception remains suspended for unattended runs; this record authorizes
  nothing for them.
- `T-20261124-007` (fleet repos ship no full-scan gitleaks config, so
  predicate 4 cannot be met there) stays open.
- Decisions of record that this ruling sits beside: `DEC-20261120-01`
  (fleet-parity carve-out) and `DEC-20261123-01` (merge when CLEAN, with the
  owner's later same-day ruling to merge #135, #258 and #240 under the outage).
