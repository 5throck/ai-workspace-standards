## Context

ADR-0105 and the v3/v4 runner prompts cite
`memory/meeting-2026-11-12-unified-batch-runner.md` as the hardening authority
record. The file does not exist and never did in the git record; the auditor raised
this at meeting 2026-11-20 (facilitator fact-check: "DOES NOT EXIST — citation points
at nothing"), and two nights of "was this dissent closed?" questions had been
answered from ADR-0105's PRESERVED DISSENT excerpt alone. The ticket offers two
options: reconstruct from artifacts, or amend the ADR and prompt to cite ADR-0105's
preserved-dissent block as the authority and drop the dead path.

## Decision

As recorded in the frontmatter: explicit supersession; no reconstruction; the
verbatim dissent blocks inside ADR-0105 are the authority of record; citation
corrections land in the 2026-11-20 governance PR (design §3.4); the 2026-11-19/20
report claims get a one-time backfill audit against origin.

Acceptance (2026-11-20): the user approved option 2 of T-20261120-004 (option b —
explicit supersession, no reconstruction) explicitly and delegated the remaining
defaults (owner delegation 2026-11-19/20). The citation corrections land in PR 1 of
the runner-hardening cluster; the backfill audit (second scope item) rides the
backfill PR (design §3.4.4).
