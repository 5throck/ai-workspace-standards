## Decision context

The 2026-10-31 hub-spoke consolidation review flagged the roster as the
un-judged piece of the topology ("220 agents stay 220 without a liaison
model") and the user ratified the liaison model in advance as decision 3 of
that meeting. Batch E⑤ (co-architect variant template creation, 2026-11-02
session) then had to adjudicate, per consumer, which of the consumed
specialists are canonical hub doctrine and which stay variant-local; its
findings (co-work owns the 5 spine agents, co-design owns the 6 design agents,
co-architect's adaptations declared as consumers — not forks) are recorded in
`hub_topology.entries`. This record pins the model so Phase 2's wave executes
a decided rule instead of re-litigating it per repo.

## Conditions attached

1. The one-liaison-per-domain cap is per consumed hub domain, not per variant:
   a variant consuming from both co-work and co-design may carry two liaisons.
2. Liaison agents are variant-identity assets: registered in `hub_topology`
   and never overwritten by promotion (design §2.3, §6.1).
3. Any change to the model itself (classes, cap, timing) returns to PM with a
   new gate-moment record; the Phase 2 wave executes this one as written.
