## Context

The first merge group ran the battery on the co-deck #259 head before merging
it, and the battery failed. Co-deck `main` already fails twelve tests (eleven
in `scripts/co-deck/tests/gen-slides-pdf-render.test.ts`, one in
`scripts/co-deck/tests/theme-visual-regression.test.ts`), and the PR head
failed those twelve plus one more: an unhandled error in
`diagram-helpers.test.ts`, because the delivered test imports an export that
the preserved helper does not have. Under DEC-20261124-01 the canary had to
merge before the other six delivery PRs, so the delivery wave stopped there.
The mirror group was not affected: its canary, co-abap #253, merged on real
CI.

## Decision

The owner's two answers are recorded as the ruling in the frontmatter.

For co-deck the comparison is by failing-test names first and by counts
second: the PR head may fail only tests that `main` fails at merge time, with
no fewer passes. For the delivery wave, co-design #89 is the canary. A
delivery PR whose own battery is non-zero is skipped and reported; the wave
stops only when a merged project fails its post-merge verification.

## Consequences

- co-deck's test suite stays red (twelve failures) after #259 merges. Those
  failures are not caused by the delivery and are not fixed by this ruling.
- co-deck #259 stays a partial delivery: the ten engine-preserved conflicts
  and the withheld `humanize` manifest entry are unchanged (T-20261124-010).
- A project whose configured test command discovers no tests (the shared test
  runner exits 0 in that case) is reported as vacuous, and a direct `bun test`
  on the PR head and on `main` must show no new failure before that PR merges.
- Every merge under this ruling keeps the ledger kind `wall-exception` and the
  marker `test-integrity: unverified`.
- DEC-20261124-01 is amended on these two points only. Nothing here
  re-authorizes the nightly runner's suspended billing-wall exception.
