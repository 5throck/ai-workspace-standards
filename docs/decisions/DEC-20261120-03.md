## Context

T-20261119-010 (owner decision 2026-11-18, option 2) required STEP I to proceed only
when STEP E ran at full scope that run. On 2026-11-19, STEP E degraded to
battery-only under its budget while STEP I delivered 14 projects anyway — systemic
findings surfaced only after the wave landed. Meeting 2026-11-20 (Issue D) showed the
arithmetic: 18 scoped reviews at 12–15 minutes far exceed E's 120-minute hard budget,
so same-run coupling starves STEP I forever; the architect conceded, and the owner
directive that day adopted freshness semantics (recorded in the ratified v4 prompt
snapshot). What remained open at the Design Gate: the gate had no mechanism in the
ADR/design of record, was not machine-checkable, and had never been observed firing.

## Decision

As recorded in the frontmatter: ledger-level freshness gate via a new
`gate-step-i` subcommand writing `gateChecks[]` rows; freshness (same HEAD, or ≤2
nights with no unresolved Critical/High since) is the admission condition;
option 2's same-run coupling is superseded while its degradation outcome is preserved
per-project. Full change spec, tests, and AC-14 are in
`docs/designs/2026-11-20-runner-hardening-integrity-design.md` §3.1.

## Consequences

As recorded in the frontmatter. Governing surfaces: `scripts/run-manifest.ts` (new
subcommand + required array), the registered 2026-11-12 design's AC-14, ADR-0105's
amended gate sentence, and the v5 prompt's STEP I (0) line — all in the same
governance change set.
