## Ruling

The `skills/` composition audit found 4 of 7 variant-scoped skills drifting between
their L0 dev-home copies and their owning-variant template copies (mece-logic-auditor
missing a section, sarif-exporter version 1.0.0 vs 1.0.1, sound-synth divergent
`l2_propagate` flags and content, swe-solve link divergence). Owner ruling: the L0
copies should be removed — variant-exclusive skills live only in their owning variant.

## Implementation (2026-08-29)

- Deleted `skills/{mece-logic-auditor,sarif-exporter,sound-synth,swe-solve}/` and their
  workspace platform mirrors (`.claude`/`.gemini`/`.agents` × 4).
- Both SKILLS.md catalogs (root + L1): rows moved from "Workspace Skills" to
  "Variant-Exclusive Skills" with the owning variant's authoritative metadata
  (sarif-exporter 1.0.1, swe-solve 1.1.1).
- Lifecycle records updated with the removal changelog entry.

## Consequences

- `skills/` now hosts only `scope: common` and `scope: workspace` skills (31 folders);
  the L0 dev-home rule applies to those only.
- Future authoring of variant-scoped skills happens directly in
  `templates/co-*/skills/` — no L0 copy, no back-sync obligation.
- The 3 in-sync variant-scoped L0 copies (accessibility-audit, presenter-mode,
  stride-threat-matrix) were kept in this pass; same removal applies if/when they
  drift, or proactively for consistency.
