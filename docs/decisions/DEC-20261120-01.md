## Context

The 2026-11-19 upgrade wave delivered `.githooks/commit-msg` and
`.github/workflows/ci.yml` updates into fleet PRs, and the runner discarded both in
co-abap, co-consult, and co-develop per the protected-paths HOLD rule while merging
the same classes of content elsewhere — a per-repo inconsistent application of a
root-authored list. Three owner asks left the policy undecided
(`tickets/governance/T-20261119-006.yaml`, status review). Meanwhile two layers
disagree about the same paths: the delivery engine classifies `.githooks/**` as LOCKED
whose pass always overwrites the project copy with the template source, and
`.github/workflows/ci.yml` as MERGE_MANAGED (both sanctioned delivery), while the
runner's protected list holds any merge touching them. The commit-msg hook carried a
real defect (T-20261116-002: first body paragraph dropped by `sed '1,/^$/d'`), fixed
template-side in PR #2065 — a fix only fleet delivery can land.

## Decision

As recorded in the frontmatter: fleet-parity carve-out with exact enumeration and
byte-identity conditions; root floor unchanged; membership changes human-only. The
full change spec, merge-predicate wording, and mechanical guard are in
`docs/designs/2026-11-20-runner-hardening-integrity-design.md` §3.6.

Acceptance (2026-11-20): accepted via the owner's standing delegation (2026-11-19/20) of
the proposed default — option (a), the fleet-parity carve-out with exact enumeration;
ratification is this change set's `docs/protected-paths.json` `fleetParityCarveOut`
landing (PR 1 of the runner-hardening cluster, human merge).

## Consequences

As recorded in the frontmatter. The governing surfaces are
`docs/protected-paths.json` (`fleetParityCarveOut` key) and the runner prompt's merge
predicate 6 (v5, cited by reference per ADR-0105 Governance); both land in one
human-merged governance PR, which is the ratification moment for this proposed
ruling.
