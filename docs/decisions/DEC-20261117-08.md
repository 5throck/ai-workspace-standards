## Context

`scripts/new-project.ts` ran a lifecycle governance pre-check that could never
block: `validate-templates.ts --json` printed 260 `TRACE` lines (invalid JSON),
the JSON was passed through argv (over the Windows 32,767-char limit even after
removing TRACE), and an unparsable payload was treated as a pass. See
[the design](../designs/2026-11-17-new-project-precheck-argv-design.md).

## Decision

The user approved the design and the O-1..O-6 defaults on 2026-11-17. This
record captures that Design Gate approval and the resulting rulings (see the
frontmatter). Versions: `validate-templates.ts` 1.84.1,
`helpers/validate-output.ts` 1.1.0, `new-project.ts` 1.45.0.

## Consequences

As recorded in the frontmatter.
