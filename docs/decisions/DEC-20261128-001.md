## Context

On 2026-11-28 the co-newbiz project filed three upstream requests
(U-20261128-003, -004, -005) describing one failure family — worktree
preparation — and two delegation tickets (T-20261128-003, T-20261128-004)
described the origin-L2 drift class. The Template Architect produced two
designs at the Design Gate Row 0, every upstream symptom having been
reproduced against the real scripts before the design was written:

- `docs/designs/2026-11-28-sync-worktree-robustness-design.md` — the
  three independent remediations R1/R2/R3 (shared canonical extends
  resolver; gitignore-aware knowledge_refs degradation;
  dev-sync self-provisioning).
- `docs/designs/2026-11-28-origin-l2-drift-design.md` — the origin-L2
  authority model, the check-drift repair, and the context-doc
  base-section seeder.

Both designs carry an open-questions section for the user approval
gate. The owner answered this session with approval.

## Decision

The approval in the frontmatter is recorded as the ruling. This record
was written by the automation-engineer at the PM's dispatch.

Both designs are approved on their recommended defaults. Each open
question keeps its text and carries a dated ruling line in its design
doc (mirroring the DEC-20261125-01 house pattern):

1. Worktree robustness: canonical-over-naive extends precedence;
   `.venv` provisioning-via-hook, nothing mirrored; the additive
   `script_manifest.provision.python` manifest field.
2. Origin-L2 drift: body-common/frontmatter-variant authority; all 7
   L2-AHEAD pairs are legitimate declared forks; `owner: pm` on co-safety
   sync stays protected variant identity; seeds-only propagation for this
   landing; stamp-advance-only for the co-design accessibility-audit pair.

Implementation sequencing (PM ruling under this approval): design A
(the worktree-robustness remediations, U-20261128-003/-004/-005) lands
first as one PR train; design B (the origin-L2 drift implementation,
T-20261128-003/-004) is dispatched as a separate job after A merges.
The design docs, their spec entries, the registry projection, and this
record land together in one docs PR before any implementation starts.

Scope limits. This decision approves the two designs and their
sequencing only. It does NOT approve: promoting any of the 7 forks,
editing delivered bytes in canonical layouts beyond what the designs
specify, or landing the untracked governance ticket yaml files (that
batch lands with its own chore PR later).

## Consequences

- The docs PR can land immediately; the spec statuses read `approved`,
  which satisfies the spec-check relevance window for the
  implementation PR train that follows.
- Design A proceeds on branch `fix/sync-worktree-robustness` with
  U-20261128-003/-004/-005 resolved against its PR on merge.
- Design B waits for its own dispatch; its approved status does not
  schedule the work by itself.
- The stale-approved-spec warning (>14 days without implementation)
  now applies to both entries — mitigated by the sequencing above.
