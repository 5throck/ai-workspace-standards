## Context

The 2026-11-19 co-utility design shipped the variant as scaffoldable and even
proved it through a `new-project --variant co-utility` e2e scaffold. The owner
then corrected the role (2026-11-21, directive 1): co-utility is the TARGET
TEMPLATE for the project-to-variant path — existing out-of-fleet projects
(e.g. QuickDL, already in-flight as the adoption side) convert into the fleet
through `adopt-project` — and a second directive the same day (adoption
projects are structurally heterogeneous; auto-upgrade/auto-backport machinery
must treat them as out-of-scope by default) produced design §5. The Design
Gate moment for the consolidated design was the user's 2026-11-21 approval of
full implementation; the landing merged the same day as PR #2137, and this
record is the retroactive DEC for that gate moment, written alongside the
governance-doc backfill (README adoption lines, skill-section updates) that
the design's §6.2 doc-touch items called for.

## Decision

As recorded in the frontmatter: full implementation approved 2026-11-21
including §5 gating; spec registered implemented (no exemption code); guard
ticket renumbered to T-20261121-004 to clear two ticket-ID collisions; gray
case #12 resolved KEEP-and-correct per user choice. Full design:
`docs/designs/2026-11-21-co-utility-dedup-and-adoption-role-design.md`
(disposition §3, gate table §5.4, curated runbook §5.5, birth-certificate
§5.6, fleet blinding §5.7). Landing evidence: PR #2137
(pr/20261121-125143-chore-templates-workspace-sync-35-files), CHANGELOG
2026-11-21 feat(co-utility) entry.
