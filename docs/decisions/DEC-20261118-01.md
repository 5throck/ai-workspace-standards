## Context

Ticket volume in the workspace-root store grew with findings that were fully
fixable inside the session that found them. On 2026-11-17 the PM triaged a
concrete case: T-20261117-014 (audit 5-site double count) was immediately
mechanically fixable, so it was deleted unfiled and routed to direct fix,
while T-20261117-012 and T-20261117-013 (deferred source-hygiene and cosmetic
work) stayed filed because their work could not land in-session. That episode
made the implicit filing practice explicit policy material.

The owner set the rule in the 2026-11-18 PM session: filing is gated, not
default. Two failure modes frame the ruling. Over-filing produces double
bookkeeping, because the landing commit or PR already records an in-session
fix. Under-filing (never file) loses work across session boundaries, which
occurred twice on 2026-11-18 through usage-limit interruptions.

Sync-embedded detection (DEC-20261117-06 batch) files only marking tickets and
leaves actual promotion to human judgment (ADR-0031), so its auto-filing
already sits inside the bar.

## Decision

As recorded in the frontmatter: the four filing criteria (deferred work,
design needed, cross-project, recurring or scheduled concern) gate every
ticket filing; in-session fixable workspace-internal items go straight to
fix, with the landing commit or PR as the record. The bar is codified as a
compact block at the top of pm-gateway-workflow.md §3.12 (Upstream Request
Triage), where PM's triage procedure lives.

## Consequences

As recorded in the frontmatter. The governing surface is
`docs/governance/agents/pm-gateway-workflow.md` §3.12; no script or schema
change accompanies this ruling.
