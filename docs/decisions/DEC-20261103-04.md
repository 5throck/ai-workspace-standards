## Decision context

The 2026-11-03 Step 2f learning pass over co-newbiz #795/#796 extracted two
gaps that are workspace-level rather than project-level. First, security-scan
v1.2.0 covers gitleaks, bun audit, hook permissions, and gitignore hygiene —
zero auth-surface checks — while route-audit-scan audits audit-token coverage,
not open signup. Yet co-newbiz #796 demonstrated the class concretely: its
register endpoint previously let anyone reaching the login page create a
default-viewer account with full business READ of the deal pipeline
(MNPI-adjacent), and was closed only by a project-local fix. Second, the
skill-graph-analytics weekly cadence had no revival home ("pending
adjudication" in the skill note) after T-20261101-004 established the
automation roster carried neither the old 02:30 runner nor any replacement.

## Rulings

1. security-scan gains the auth-surface audit step (v1.3.0). The finding
   definition is deliberately role-based, not endpoint-based: an unauthenticated
   signup that grants any authenticated role is the risk (anyone reaching the
   login page can mint an identity and inherit that role's reads). The
   reference remediation shape is the co-newbiz #796 five-point pattern
   (default-closed flag, refusal before body-parse/throttle, dated
   owner-decision comment, admin-provisioned accounts, documented
   reversibility).
2. The revival home for the weekly skill-graph analytics is the 01:30 fleet
   runner's FRIDAY FULL OVERRIDE (decided 2026-11-03, T-20261101-004; runner
   prompt updated via automation tooling). The skill note records the decision
   and drops the "pending adjudication" phrasing (v1.3.1).

## Landing

Both rulings land in one batch-delivery PR (T-20261103-006): the two skill
updates, this decision record, the regenerated VERSION_MANIFEST rows
(security-scan 1.3.0, skill-graph-analytics 1.3.1), and the regenerated
docs/skill-graph.json/.md projections (verify via
`bun scripts/verify-skill-graph.ts`).
