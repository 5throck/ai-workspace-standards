## Decision context

The evidence-backport scanner (design 2026-09-19, ADR-0084) has reported
`Projects/co-safety` PROMOTABLE for three consecutive nightly cycles, and
by design that verdict routes to a human-triage row rather than to any
automatic promotion — the Step 2b promotion is a human judgment over a
machine candidate. The row could not close itself: the design deliberately
gives the scanner no authority to promote. The user's 2026-10-30 directive
to backport the three projects supplies the missing human input, and this
record fixes its meaning before any authoring dispatch starts, so the
implementing specialist executes against a settled contract. Status
`accepted` records the gate-moment ruling; the pair authoring it authorizes
proceeds as a normal PM dispatch, and the dispositions of the full
user-directed cycle land in the cycle report.

## Conditions attached

1. The pair contract is binding and verbatim: F3 schema + generalized
   `evidence-collection-<name>` skill + provenance README, in one change
   under `templates/co-safety/`. Review rejects a schema without its
   companion procedure.
2. The generalized skill preserves step sequence, record-ID convention,
   and status/closure discipline, and drops engagement-specific scope,
   client identifiers, and project-local tool paths. Engagement content
   never lands template-side (Safety Rule 6, ADR-0031).
3. The README must carry the M1–M6 table with the M2/M4 verification
   method stated — the git-log fallback over the measured commit range
   this cycle — and must name the commit range measured.
4. Nothing auto-writes into `templates/`; the promotion is authored at
   review time under the PM Gateway, and the cycle report carries the
   per-pair dispositions for co-deck and co-abap as well.
