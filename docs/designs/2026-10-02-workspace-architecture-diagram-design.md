# Design: integrated workspace architecture diagram

- **Spec ID**: `2026-10-02-workspace-architecture-diagram`
- **Date**: 2026-10-02
- **Status**: implemented
- **Scope**: `docs/architecture/workspace-architecture{,.en,.es,.ja}.svg`; copies as Appendix E in `multi-agent-harness-handbook` and Appendix G in `intro-to-ai-harness` (separate repositories). No runtime code changes.

## 1. Problem

There was no single integrated view of the workspace. The L0 to L3 layer stack, the
distribution and upgrade flows, backporting and promotion, upstream requests, the ticket
system, and the co-workspace service were each described in separate ADRs and designs.
Readers could not see how they connect or where the loops close.

## 2. Deliverables

- `docs/architecture/workspace-architecture.svg` (Korean source) plus `.en.svg`, `.es.svg`,
  `.ja.svg` with identical layout; only prose labels are translated, identifiers, file
  names, commands, and ADR ids stay verbatim.
- Handbook copies: Appendix E (`E_Workspace_Architecture*`) in `multi-agent-harness-handbook`
  and Appendix G (`G_Workspace_Architecture*`) in `intro-to-ai-harness`, four languages each,
  with the matching SVG inlined per language, an "open full size" link, and in-page zoom.
  Delivered by 5throck/multi-agent-harness-handbook#168 and 5throck/intro-to-ai-harness#70
  (both merged).

## 3. Requirements

- R1: One diagram shows L0 to L3, upgrade, backport and promotion, upstream request,
  tickets, co-workspace, and the governance control plane.
- R2: Flows are color-coded (distribution, upgrade, backport, upstream, ticket, governance)
  with a legend; a numbered strip lists the easy-to-miss points.
- R3: Content stays within facts verified from the repo (CONSTITUTION, ADR-0031/0092/0097,
  `docs/designs/*`); no invented behavior.
- R4: All four language variants fit their boxes without overflow.

## 4. Accessibility

- Each SVG has `<title>` and `<desc>`; inline copies add `role="img"` and `aria-label`.
- Text is at least 14px; text on fills meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast.
- Zoom controls (+, -, reset) are native buttons with localized accessible names, keyboard
  operable; the scroll viewport is focusable.
- Handbook a11y, structure (inline-SVG rule), link, and i18n-parity validators pass.

## 5. Verification

Rendered with `qlmanage` and `xmllint --noout` per variant. The pages were also rendered in
a browser, and the zoom controls were exercised on the Spanish page.

## 6. Known limits

- The generator (`/tmp/g/gen3.py` and its string table) is not in the repository; the SVGs
  are the maintained artifacts and must be edited directly or regenerated by a new tool.
- Handbook copies are byte-identical to these SVGs by design: the two handbook
  repositories deploy independently and cannot reference files in this repository.
- The right edge of the en, es, and ja renders was only checked by the text-fit estimator,
  because the preview thumbnail cropped about 3 to 5 percent of it.

## 6a. Cleanup

Two stray files, `docs/architecture/workspace-architecture-en.svg` and `.png`, were swept
into main by an earlier scoped-staging commit; they are removed as superseded by the
`.en.svg` naming.
